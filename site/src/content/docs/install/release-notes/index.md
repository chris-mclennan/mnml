## Earlier versions

mnml 0.2.x was the Rust generation of mnml. Its releases, from the
first through 0.2.22, are archived with their binaries at
[chris-mclennan/mnml-rust](https://github.com/chris-mclennan/mnml-rust/releases).
Every release, old and new, is also listed on
[GitHub](https://github.com/chris-mclennan/mnml/releases).
