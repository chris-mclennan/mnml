mnml 0.3.4 is a follow-up release to 0.3.3, and its only change is that
its Marketplace index names Jira and Bitbucket 0.2.3. An installed mnml
offers the integrations its own release was cut with, and 0.3.3 was cut
before 0.2.3 of either existed. With 0.3.4, the Jira and Bitbucket work
described under [0.3.3](/docs/install/release-notes/0-3-3) reaches a
Marketplace install. Nothing else changes.

This release is 1 contributor over 18 commits since 0.3.3.

## What it unlocks

Jira and Bitbucket 0.2.3 carry the integration side of what 0.3.3
describes:

- **The recent-items cache.** Both integrations write it as a side
  effect of their polls, so a ticket key or pull request hovers with its
  title and *Recent tickets and pull requests* has something to list.
  See [What a link names](/docs/features/links#what-a-link-names).
- **Pull-request and pipeline number ranges.** Bitbucket publishes the
  ranges a bare `PR #5505` or `pipeline 10554` resolves against. See
  [Pull-request references](/docs/features/links#pull-request-references).
- **Atlassian chip icons**, such as the Jira Work chip's work-items
  card with a check. Run `./run.sh install-font` to pick up the glyph.
- **The confirm-box click fix.** A click on OK or Cancel in Jira's and
  Bitbucket's merge confirms lands on the chip, not the box behind it.
- **Measured tables.** A wide pane gives its spare width to the columns
  being cut instead of leaving it blank.

The full entry is the
[v0.3.4 section of CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/chris-mclennan/mnml/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#v034)
and the [GitHub release](https://github.com/chris-mclennan/mnml/releases/tag/v0.3.4).

## Upgrading

Homebrew, winget and the installers move you to 0.3.4 on your next
upgrade:

```sh
brew upgrade mnml
```

```powershell
winget upgrade ChrisMcLennan.mnml
```

Or run the installer for your platform again, from
[Install](/docs/install). winget is Microsoft's community repository and
merges each version on its own schedule, so 0.3.4 can reach it some time
after the other channels.

Then open the Marketplace tab under INTEGRATIONS: an installed Jira or
Bitbucket 0.2.2 reads *update available*. See
[Marketplace](/docs/integrations/marketplace#updating).
